## 产品描述（Long，500字以内）

Visora is a GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) tool that helps indie hackers and small SaaS teams get cited by AI search engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Claude.

AI search traffic grew 500%+ in 2025. If your brand isn't appearing in AI-generated answers, you're invisible to a growing segment of potential customers. Visora fixes that.

**How it works:**
1. Enter your website and up to 5 competitors
2. Visora sends 50 relevant questions to AI platforms and tracks every citation
3. You see exactly which competitors appear — and which third-party sources are causing it
4. Visora generates ready-to-use content: FAQ pages, Schema markup, and platform submission lists to close the gap

**Who it's for:**
- Indie hackers who built a product but can't figure out why ChatGPT never recommends it
- Small SaaS teams transitioning from SEO to AI search
- Founders who want to understand their AI visibility before competitors do

**What makes Visora different:**
Unlike monitoring-only tools (Otterly, Rankscale), Visora adds execution — it tells you exactly what to do and generates the content to do it. Pricing starts at $29/month, making professional GEO accessible without enterprise budgets.
